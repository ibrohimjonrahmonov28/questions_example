English Language Proficiency · Aviation

Speak your answers. The examiner is listening.

The examiner reads every question aloud. You get a few seconds to think, then press SPEAK and answer before the time runs out. At the end you get an ICAO level estimate (1–6) based on your speaking, and a report in Uzbek with your errors corrected.

Your role

    Parts 1 and 2: read the question or look at the picture, then press the red SPEAK button when you are ready. Parts 3 and 4: the examiner reads aloud, you get a few seconds to think, then press SPEAK. Recording stops when the time runs out, when you stop talking, or when you press Stop and send.

    The examiner needs Claude. Open this page on claude.ai while signed in to your Claude account.
    Voice answers are not available in this view. Open the link in Chrome (Android or computer) to answer by voice. You can still take the exam by typing, with the same timer.
    Exam structure
    1. P1Introduction: 3–4 questions. Read each question, press SPEAK when ready, answer.
    2. P2Pictures: describe a standard picture for 1 minute, then compare it with a non-standard picture for 1 minute.
    3. P3Listening: an incident report and a radio exchange, read aloud once. The transcript stays folded.
    4. P4RT role-play: routine calls, a developing abnormal situation, then an urgent safety conflict.

    Use headphones so the examiner's voice does not reach the microphone. To hear a question again, say “Say again” or “Could you repeat the question?” in your answer, as you would in the real exam.
