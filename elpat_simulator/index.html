Sit a full ELPAT with a live examiner

Four parts, one step at a time: introduction, pictures and discussion, listening, and a radiotelephony role-play. At the end you get an ICAO level estimate (1–6) and a report in Uzbek with your errors corrected.

Your role
The examiner needs Claude. Open this page on claude.ai while signed in to your Claude account.
Exam structure
  1. P1Introduction: your job, background and routine operations.
  2. P2Pictures: describe a normal scene, compare an abnormal one, ask the examiner three questions, then discuss.
  3. P3Listening: an incident report and a radio exchange. Tap LISTEN to hear them read aloud where your browser supports it.
  4. P4RT role-play: routine calls, a developing abnormal situation, then an urgent safety conflict.

Answer in English. The examiner will not correct you during the test. Use the microphone button to speak your answers where it is offered.
