English Language Proficiency · Aviation

Speak your answers. The examiner is listening.

The examiner reads every question aloud. You get a few seconds to get ready, then the microphone captures your answer until the time runs out. At the end you get an ICAO level estimate (1–6) based on your speaking, and a report in Uzbek with your errors corrected.

Your role

Starting the exam turns on the microphone. It stays on until the report; only what you say during the red SPEAK NOW window is recorded.

The examiner needs Claude. Open this page on claude.ai while signed in to your Claude account.
Voice answers are not available in this view. Open the link in Chrome (Android or computer) to answer by voice. You can still take the exam by typing, with the same timer.
Exam structure
  1. P1Introduction: your job, background and routine operations.
  2. P2Pictures: describe a normal scene, compare an abnormal one, ask the examiner three questions, then discuss.
  3. P3Listening: an incident report and a radio exchange, read aloud once. The transcript stays folded.
  4. P4RT role-play: routine calls, a developing abnormal situation, then an urgent safety conflict.

Use headphones so the examiner's voice does not reach the microphone. To hear a question again, say “Say again” or “Could you repeat the question?” in your answer, as you would in the real exam.
