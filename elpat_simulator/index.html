English Language Proficiency · Aviation Test

A full ELPAT, spoken and timed

Four parts in about 25–30 minutes. The examiner speaks every question. You press ANSWER, speak, and press STOP. At the end you get an indicative ICAO level for each of the six criteria.

    Examination structure
    1. P1Warm-up: questions about you and your work.2–3 min
    2. P2Pictures: describe one (1 min), compare two (2 min), ask three questions, discuss.8–10 min
    3. P3Listening: two radio recordings with background noise, max. two plays each, note-taking, questions.8–10 min
    4. P4RT role-play: standard calls, abnormal situations, safety conflicts. Readbacks are checked.10–15 min

    Use headphones in Parts 3 and 4. The examiner never interrupts you and gives no corrections until the final report.

    Photo bank for Part 2 (optional)

    Add real aviation photographs as pairs: a normal situation and an abnormal one in the same kind of setting. Claude views each pair once and writes hidden examiner notes. Use photos you have the right to use.
